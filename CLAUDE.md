## Copy Non-Negotiables (All Brands)
- Australian spelling throughout
- No em dashes (Ardour is the exception — em dashes are correct for Ardour)
- No hyperbole
- No pretentious language
- No comparative or correlative structures ("Whether X or Y", "From X to Y")
- No directive openers ("Celebrate", "Experience", "Discover")
- No generic openers ("Nestled...", "Here —")
- No prose ending with "Here..."
- No overused words: scrumptious, delectable, indulge, elevated, culinary journey, beauty
- Factual USPs over vague emotional claims
- Tone: elegant, grounded, warm — without unnecessary prepositional phrases
- SEO copy: written line by line with non-negotiables applied continuously
- Meta Ads cold audiences: elevated, evocative, factual

## ICP Summary

**Spicers:** Julie (45+, affluent, rewarder) and Sam (25–44, ambitious, earner). Quality-seekers. Nature, food, wine, connection. Never respond to discount language.
**Ardour:** The Celebrant — social, food and wine-led, occasion-motivated. Wants warmth and character, not formality.
**Bannisters Hotels:** Coastal Escapee — F&B-motivated, social, Sydney-based, long weekend mindset. Mollymook skews polished coastal; Port Stephens ("Barefoot Luxury") skews casual and playful. Rick Stein at Bannisters is a core Mollymook differentiator.
**Worlds Apart:** Rewards program (Rewards, Wellness, Dining, Events). Retargeting audiences significantly outperform cold audiences. Cold = awareness only. Tagline: "A world of rewards that stand apart."
