# Brand: Ardour Hotels & Estates

## Overview
Ardour Hotels & Estates is a curated collection of Australian luxury hotel destinations, launched in 2026 under Salter Brothers Hospitality. The flagship property is Ardour Milton Park Bowral (Southern Highlands NSW), which reopened February 2026 following a multi-million dollar restoration. Upcoming properties include Lilianfels Blue Mountains and Kingsford The Barossa. Properties are bookable through worldsapart.club.

## Positioning
A handpicked collection of Australian destinations where luxury feels warm, social, and alive. Guided by the spirit of the ultimate host, Ardour creates places that invite connection, encourage spontaneity, and celebrate life's moments big and small.

**Brand tagline direction:** "A new way to celebrate, together"

## Properties (current and pipeline)
- Ardour Milton Park Bowral — Southern Highlands NSW (44 rooms, flagship, open Feb 2026)
- Ardour Lilianfels Blue Mountains — pipeline, mid-2026 (currently operating as Lilianfels Blue Mountains)
- Ardour Kingsford The Barossa — pipeline (currently operating as Kingsford The Barossa)

Note: Lilianfels Blue Mountains and Kingsford The Barossa are transitioning into the Ardour brand. Until rebranding is complete, treat them as operating under their existing identities but within the Ardour family for planning purposes.

## Brand Personality
The Ultimate Host. Confident, warm, proactive, spontaneous. Anticipates needs before guests know they have them. Sharp, effortlessly tempting, always inviting. Gives guests full permission to be and do whatever they want.

## Tone of Voice
**Voice persona:** Like that friend who curates the perfect weekend away.

**Principles:**
1. Luxury tone that never tries too hard — warm, confident, easy-going. Short clear sentences. No jargon.
2. Don't ask for permission, give it — offer suggestions and invitations, not pressure.
3. Specific not generic — sensory storytelling. Zoom in on actual details (thread count, wine flavour, birdsong).

**Do:** Speak in first person to guests. First person plural (we/our/us) in marketing. Be specific about feelings and moments. Use idioms for wit. Show personality. Allude to luxury. Write how you speak.
**Don't:** Speak in third person. Be arrogant. Use generalisations or platitudes. Use puns. Say "rich", "bougie" or "expensive". Use tired constructions (Goodbye city, hello countryside). Describe things as "iconic." Be too formal.

**Tone scale target:** The Ultimate Host — effortless, charming, confident. Relaxed, warm, human. Specific, sensorial, evocative. Elevated, refined, indulgent. Helpful, positive, active.

## Language Rules
- Australian spelling (organise, colour, travelled)
- Numbers written as digits (3 things to do while you stay)
- Spell out "and" not "&"
- Always use a full stop
- Dates: Friday, 1 August 2025 (not 1st August or 01/08)
- Avoid exclamation marks
- Use em dash (—) not en dash (–)
- No emojis (exception: one in email subject lines for open rate)
- Use contractions (you'll, we're, it's)
- Favour full stops over commas for sharpness

## Design
**Colours:**
- Ardour Red — Pantone 2349U, RGB 182/61/36, HEX #B63D24
- Cream — Pantone 9043U, RGB 233/224/207, HEX #E9E0CF
- Slate — Pantone 404U, RGB 109/110/105, HEX #6D6E69
- Black — #000000
- White — #FFFFFF

**Typography:**
- TWK Ghost (Regular and Italics) — headlines and body copy, upper/title/lower case
- Venus SB Extended (Medium, capitals only) — headings and small paragraphs
- Arial — cross-platform fallback (emails)
- Tracking: +100 to +150 when set in uppercase

**Logo:** ARDOUR wordmark (all caps, Venus SB Extended). Monogram (A cipher in circle) used independently on-property. Destination logotype adds property name below (e.g. ARDOUR / MILTON PARK BOWRAL).

**Pattern:** Geometric diamond repeat — used full-bleed only, never constrained.

## Sensitivities
- Never compare to competitors
- Not elitist or exclusive — welcoming of all guests
- Not overly formal or stiff
- Not transactional or cold
- Avoid tired hospitality clichés
