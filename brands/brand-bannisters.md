# Brand: Bannisters Hotels

## Overview
Bannisters Hotels is a coastal luxury hotel group under the Salter Brothers Hospitality umbrella. Properties are located along the NSW coastline and are bookable through worldsapart.club.

**Platform tagline:** "Boutique hotels with a food & beverage soul"

**Brand tagline:** "Better at Bannisters" (used as an accent/script element on collateral)

---

## Properties

### Mollymook
- **Bannisters by the Sea** — the flagship Mollymook hotel. Infinity pool, ocean views, Rick Stein at Bannisters restaurant.
- **Bannisters Pavilion Mollymook Beach** — second Mollymook property. More contemporary interiors. Different audience energy to By the Sea.

*When advertising both Mollymook properties together, use the Bannisters Hotels Mollymook group logo.*

### Port Stephens
- **Bannisters Port Stephens** — coastal property with a distinct identity. Brand positioning: "Barefoot Luxury."

### Venues
- **Rick Stein at Bannisters** — seafood restaurant. Always referred to as "Rick Stein at Bannisters," never just Rick Stein. Signature logo always paired with "AT BANNISTERS."
- **The Rooftop** — venue at Bannisters by the Sea. Sub-venues: The Upper Deck, Rooftop East.
- **Pool Bar at Bannisters** — by the sea, Mollymook.

---

## Positioning
Coastal luxury with a food and beverage heart. Bannisters properties are known for their ocean settings, relaxed luxury, and dining-led guest experience. Positioned for travellers seeking a blend of relaxation and energy — different in character from the retreat-focused Spicers brand.

Port Stephens is specifically positioned as **Barefoot Luxury** — a slightly more casual, sandy, sun-drenched energy compared to Mollymook's more polished coastal feel.

---

## Tone & Voice

**Personality:** Relaxed coastal energy. Food and beverage-forward. Unpretentious. Convivial. Driven by the pleasure of good food, good wine, and great company.

**What it sounds like:**
- Warm and inviting, with a coastal ease
- F&B-led: specific dishes, produce, chef context where relevant
- Social and shareable
- Shorter sentences, lighter touch than Spicers

**What it does not sound like:**
- Corporate or stiff
- Over-polished generic luxury
- Retreat-focused (that's Spicers territory)

## Logo System

### Group Logos
- **Bannisters Hotels** — used when advertising both Port Stephens and Mollymook together
- **Bannisters Hotels Mollymook** — used when advertising Mollymook only

### Property Logos
- **Bannisters by the Sea** — Mollymook flagship
- **Bannisters Pavilion Mollymook Beach** — distinct script-style wordmark
- **Bannisters Port Stephens** — distinct from Mollymook marks

### Icon / Monogram
- **B|H** — used for social profile images, favicons, and small-format applications where the full wordmark is not legible

### Venue Logos
- **Rick Stein at Bannisters** — Rick Stein signature script always used with "AT BANNISTERS." Can be used alongside group hotel logos.
- **The Rooftop** — script logo. Used with Bannisters Hotels or Bannisters Hotels Mollymook logos only. Never paired with Bannisters Pavilion logo.
- **The Upper Deck** — circular mark, used in black or white only
- **Rooftop East** — circular mark, used in black or white only
- **Pool Bar at Bannisters** — script mark. Used with Bannisters Hotels or Bannisters Hotels Mollymook logos only. Not to be used with Pavilion logo.

### Colour Application Rules
- All hotel and group logos: Bannisters navy, white, or black only
- Rick Stein and Pool Bar logos: white or coloured version only
- B|H icon: Bannisters navy or white only

### Alignment Rules
- Logos must always sit at 180 degrees (horizontal). Never diagonal or rotated 90 degrees.
- Logos should be centred, or aligned to the far left or far right
- Logos must never be cut off
- Only one logo should be used per artwork

## Typography

### Header
- **Font:** Montserrat Semibold (Medium depending on screen render)
- **Case:** All capitals only
- **Colour:** Bannisters navy, black, or white
- **Tracking:** 200–320pt
- **Leading:** Font size + 15%
- **Note:** Headers should not run over two lines where possible

### Sub-Header
- **Font:** Montserrat Light or Regular (depending on readability)
- **Case:** All capitals only
- **Colour:** Black or white
- **Tracking:** 200–320pt
- **Leading:** 175% of font size (1.75× font size)
- **Note:** May also serve as main header on collateral where needed. Sub-headers in navy is incorrect — black or white only.

### Body Copy
- **Font:** Source Sans Pro Light / Light Italic
- Regular and Italic permitted where extra legibility is required (e.g. mobile, newspaper ads)
- **Colour:** #5A5A5A (copy grey) or white
- **Alignment:** Left-aligned (justified in print) or centred
- **Tracking:** 0–50pt
- **Leading:** Natural, generous — open-spaced feel

### Accent Font
- **Font:** Portrait Script Regular
- **Case:** Sentence case only — never all caps
- **Colour:** Black, white, or Bannisters blue. Dusky Pink permitted for Port Stephens collateral only.
- **Tracking:** Zero
- **Leading:** 3× text size
- **Note:** Must always be large enough to be legible

### Type Layout Hierarchy
- Header: 2–3× the size of body copy. Centred or flush left.
- Sub-header: 1.5× the size of body copy. Same alignment as header.
- Body: Justified left (print) or left/centre aligned (web)
- Accent: Centred or right-aligned, offset past the body copy edge

## Photography Style

**Bannisters Hotels:**
- Capture the Bannisters experience — not overly sexual, more classic
- Emotive with a summer vacation feel
- Full-bleed bright images are the focal point of every design piece
- Text in design sits in white space and draws guests into the experience
- Refer to the Bannisters Hotel Social Media Guide for full imagery guidelines

**Rick Stein at Bannisters:**
- Capture the Rick Stein experience
- Images should reflect the essence of "fresh seafood, simply cooked"
- Refer to the Rick Stein Social Media Guide for full imagery guidelines

---

## Graphic Style

**Images are the hero of the brand.**
- Full-bleed, bright images are the focal point of every piece of design (print and digital)
- Text in design sits in white space — it draws guests into the experience, never dominates the image
- Design should be modern, minimal, and clean
- Remove any element that is not needed

---

## Brand Elements

**Splash:**
- A scattered dot/droplet motif used in Ocean Spray Blue or white
- Used only once per piece of collateral
- Used as a small element only — never dominant

## Key Marketing Context
- Part of the Worlds Apart platform alongside Spicers, Ardour, and Independents
- Target audience overlaps with Spicers (affluent, 25–55, food-motivated) but skews coastal and social vs nature/retreat
- Port Stephens has a more casual, playful energy (Barefoot Luxury) compared to Mollymook's polished coastal feel
- Rick Stein partnership is a core brand differentiator for Mollymook
