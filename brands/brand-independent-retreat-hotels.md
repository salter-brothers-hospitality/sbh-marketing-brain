# Brand: Independent Retreat Hotels

## Overview
Independent Retreat Hotels is a collection of independently characterful hotels under the Salter Brothers Hospitality umbrella, surfaced through the Worlds Apart booking platform.

**Platform tagline:** "A collection of hotels, crafted with character"

## Known Properties
Properties vary — the "Independent" designation covers hotels that sit outside the defined Spicers, Ardour, or Bannisters brand families but are part of the SBH portfolio.

## Brand Guidelines Status
No brand guidelines exist for Independent Retreat Hotels. The collection is defined by the diversity and individual character of its properties rather than a unified brand identity.

## Positioning (inferred from platform)
Each property stands on its own character. The "Independent" label signals differentiation — these are not cookie-cutter luxury hotels. The unifying idea is craftsmanship and character over brand conformity.

## Tone Notes
- Property-specific rather than brand-wide
- Copy should lead with what makes each property distinct
- Avoid applying Spicers or Ardour tone directly — each property deserves its own voice
- The Worlds Apart platform tone is the safe default for cross-property copy

## Key Marketing Context
- Bookable through Worlds Apart alongside the other SBH brands
- Marketing should lean into the individuality of each property
- Likely audience: travellers who seek authentic, characterful stays over brand recognition

This file should be updated as individual properties within the collection are identified and briefed. There are currently no brand guidelines to reference. All copy should be developed from property-level context and briefed accordingly.
