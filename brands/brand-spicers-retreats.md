# Brand: Spicers Retreats

## Overview
Spicers Retreats is a collection of luxury boutique retreats across Queensland and New South Wales, acquired by Salter Brothers Hospitality in 2023. The brand sits within the Worlds Apart booking platform alongside Ardour, Bannisters, and Independent Retreat Hotels.

## Positioning
**Brand belief:** Luxury is best when it feels relaxed. "Relaxed Luxury" — a relaxed HOME with luxury HOTEL service.
**Purpose:** We create quality experiences where life can be celebrated and shared.

## Properties
- Spicers Balfour Hotel — New Farm, Brisbane QLD
- Spicers Clovelly Estate — Montville, Sunshine Coast Hinterland QLD
- Spicers Tamarind Retreat — Maleny, Sunshine Coast QLD
- Spicers Hidden Vale — Grandchester QLD (12,000 acres)
- Spicers Peak Lodge — Maryvale, Scenic Rim QLD
- Spicers Vineyards Estate — Hunter Valley NSW
- Spicers Potts Point — Potts Point, Sydney NSW
- Spicers Guesthouse — Hunter Valley NSW (45 rooms)
- Spicers Sangoma Retreat — Bowen Mountain, Blue Mountains NSW (all-inclusive)

**Eco Active:** Spicers Scenic Rim Trail, Canopy, Hidden Peaks, Amphitheatre, Timber Getters Eco Camp, Mount Mistake Farmhouse

**Restaurants:** The Long Apron, The Tamarind, Homage, eRemo, Restaurant Botanica, The Balfour Kitchen & Bar, The Peak, Amara

## Brand Personality
Warm, knowledgeable, lighthearted. Food and wine obsessed. Nature-connected. Caring. Generous of spirit. Does not take itself too seriously.

## Values
- Happy — sense of fun, advocates for the brand
- Humble — listens, no ego, values teamwork
- Smart — empathic, efficient, business-first

## Target Audience
**'Julie'** — 45+, affluent, frequent traveller, frequent diner, values quality, down-to-earth, active, outdoorsy
**'Sam'** — 25–44, affluent, ambitious, socially conscious, fitness conscious, leisure travel interest

**Occasions:** Birthdays, anniversaries, proposals/honeymoons, babymoons, girls getaways, active getaways, corporate retreats, meetings and events

## Brand Attributes
High quality. Luxurious. Feels relaxed. Incredible food. Perfect for special occasions. Cares for the environment. Great way to get back to nature. Home away from home. Not pretentious. Not boring.

## Tone of Voice
**Inviting (warm):** Friendly, honest, genuine. Real language from one person to another.
**Lighthearted:** Enthusiastic with subtlety and good taste. Willing to use a pun. Uses idioms not colloquialisms.
**Knowledgeable:** Specific not general. Leadership language. Shares knowledge generously.

**Do:** Recommend, suggest, encourage. Create subtle urgency. Use words like welcome, join us, consider, visit. Use contractions.
**Don't:** Use words like "yummy", slang, sarcasm. Brag. Make comparisons with competitors. Use pretentious language. Overtell.

## Content Guidelines
- Australian spelling throughout
- No capitals in headlines (exception: retreat names)
- Contractions encouraged
- Numbers: zero–nine written out, 10+ as numerals
- Times: 12-hour clock, lowercase, no space (9.00am, 5.00pm)
- Dates: day, date, month, year (15 July 2018)
- Ampersands only in titles or space-limited contexts
- Website: spicersretreats.com (no www, no full stop)
- Wi-Fi (not WIFI or wifi)

## Design
**Primary colour:** Umber — HEX #5a514e
**Secondary colour:** Leaf green — HEX #73932C
**Typography:** Montserrat (Thin/Regular for headlines, Regular/Light for body, Bold for subheads)

## Imagery
Professionally shot. Clean, uncluttered spaces. People relaxed and having fun — not staged. Natural daylight. Colour lightly processed, not over-saturated. Handheld video feel — organic, not stifled.

## Sustainability
Committed to zero net waste and zero emissions by 2030. Leaf green used to identify sustainability initiatives in guest-facing collateral.

## Sensitivities
- Never use pretentious language
- No hyperbole ("our food is amazing")
- No comparisons with competitors
- No slang
- Not too formal
