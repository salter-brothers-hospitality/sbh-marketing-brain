# Brand: Worlds Apart

## Overview
Worlds Apart is the master booking platform and loyalty brand for Salter Brothers Hospitality. It acts as the unified front-end for all SBH brands — Spicers Retreats, Ardour Hotels & Estates, Bannisters, and Independent Retreat Hotels.

**Website:** worldsapart.club
**Tagline:** "A world of rewards that stand apart"
**Brand credential:** "A Worlds Apart Experience" — used on Ardour, Spicers, and future brands to reference Worlds Apart

---

## Role in Portfolio
Worlds Apart is not a hotel brand — it is the discovery, booking, and loyalty layer that sits above all SBH brands. It serves as:
- A unified booking gateway across 19+ properties
- The primary digital touchpoint for cross-brand promotions and offers
- The platform through which member rewards and loyalty-style benefits are surfaced
- A rewards program with categories: Rewards, Wellness, Dining, and Events

**Sub-brand taglines on platform:**
- Ardour Hotels & Estates — "A new way to celebrate, together"
- Bannisters — "Boutique hotels with a food & beverage soul"
- Spicers Retreats — "Luxury retreats in unique settings"
- Independent Retreat Hotels — "A collection of hotels, crafted with character"

## Logo System

### Variations
- **Primary Logo** — Worlds Apart wordmark with line element and "A World of Rewards That Stand Apart" credential. Default for most applications.
- **Wordmark** — wordmark only, without the credential. Use alongside Primary in most applications.
- **Brand Mark** — W-A monogram. Use when size is a concern or for social media profile images.
- **Brand Credential** — "A Worlds Apart Experience" — applied to Ardour, Spicers, and future brand collateral to reference the platform.
- **Extended Logo** — line element stretches horizontally for wider formats.
- **Condensed Logo** — line element shortened for narrow formats.

### Line Element
The horizontal line element connecting "Worlds" and "Apart" can flex in length to suit different formats. All other elements remain consistent with supplied logo files.

### Colour Variants
All logo variants are available in Charcoal and Monowhite.

### Sizing Rules
- Primary Logo minimum: 14mm (print) / 53px (digital)
- Wordmark minimum: 4mm (print) / 15px (digital)
- Brand Mark minimum: 3mm (print) / 11px (digital)
- For smaller sizes, use the Wordmark or Brand Mark instead of the Primary Logo

### Clear Space
Clear space around the logo = X on all sides, where X equals the height of the "W" in the Worlds Apart wordmark. No text or graphics should enter this area.

## Tone and Positioning

Elevated but accessible. The tone mirrors the aspirational positioning of the portfolio without being brand-specific. Worlds Apart should feel like a trusted curator — knowledgeable, warm, and confident in its recommendations.

**What it sounds like:**
- Evocative but brief
- Property and experience-forward
- "Your shortcut to somewhere better"
- Cross-brand without feeling generic

**What it does not sound like:**
- Promotional or discount-driven
- Overly brand-specific (not too Spicers, not too Ardour)
- Transactional or functional

---

## Key Marketing Context
- Used as the booking destination for cross-brand Meta Ads and Google Ads campaigns
- Properties appear on the platform under their respective brand sub-sections
- Offers and packages are surfaced per property through the platform
- The Coolcations campaign and similar multi-property campaigns route to Worlds Apart

---

## Sensitivities
- Worlds Apart is a platform brand, not a hotel brand — copy should not position it as a place in itself
- Tone should feel brand-agnostic — not overly Spicers, Ardour, or Bannisters specific
- Must feel premium and editorial, not promotional or discount-driven
