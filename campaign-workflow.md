# Campaign Workflow

## Campaign Initiation
Campaigns are initiated through a mix of sources:
- **Strategic:** Planned campaigns originating from the marketing calendar, seasonal strategy, or brand priorities
- **Reactive:** Campaigns initiated in response to short-term revenue or occupancy priorities

Campaigns should be structured modularly where possible to accommodate adjustments without requiring full rebuilds.

---

## Approval Process
- **Primary approver:** Marketing leadership
- Property and GM input may be required for property-specific campaigns but final sign-off sits with marketing leadership
- Approvals are centralised — no external agency in the approval chain

---

## Campaign Structure (Brief to Launch)

### 1. Campaign Brief
All campaigns begin with a detailed multi-section brief. Brief should cover:

- **Campaign name**
- **Objective** — what the campaign needs to achieve (bookings, awareness, lead gen, etc.)
- **Background and context** — why this campaign is happening, what problem it solves
- **Target audience** — specific segment, persona, or retargeting pool
- **Key message** — the single most important thing the audience should take away
- **Offer or hook** — package, rate, experience, or seasonal peg
- **Channels in play** — paid social, organic, email, SEO, programmatic, etc.
- **Creative requirements** — formats, sizes, video vs static, copy needs
- **Timeline** — campaign dates, key milestones, launch date
- **Budget** — total and channel allocation
- **KPIs** — how success will be measured
- **Approvals required** — who needs to sign off and by when
- **Assets needed from property** — photography, copy inputs, rate information

### 2. Creative Production
- Copy and creative developed centrally against the brief
- Copy non-negotiables applied at production stage (Australian spelling, no em dashes for non-Ardour brands, no directive openers, no hyperbole, etc.)
- Adobe Creative Suite and Canva used for asset production — Canva is the primary tool for file sharing and collaboration across teams
- Reels/video is primary format for social creative
- Static assets produced for paid placements in required sizes
- All assets delivered centrally

### 3. Approvals
- All campaigns and creative approved centrally through marketing leadership
- Any property-submitted components (rates, offer details, property-specific assets) are approved up the chain before being incorporated
- Build adequate buffer time into campaign timelines to allow for review cycles

### 4. Platform Setup
- Meta: campaigns set up within the relevant property ad account, structured by objective or seasonal campaign, with ad sets broken down by audience and ad content
- Pinterest and TikTok: set up at brand level
- Google Ads: campaign and ad group structure aligned to search intent
- Programmatic (StackAdapt, Quantcast): trafficking and pixel verification via GTM
- Email: built and scheduled in Revinate (migrating to Agilysys ADM)
- GTM used for all tracking implementation and pixel verification before launch

### 5. Launch and QA
- All tracking verified before launch
- Creative checked across placements and devices
- UTM parameters applied consistently for attribution

### 6. In-Campaign Management
- Performance monitored against KPIs
- Budget pacing checked regularly
- Creative refreshed as needed to combat fatigue (particularly on Meta)
- Reactive adjustments made in response to performance data or leadership direction

### 7. Reporting
- Campaign-level reporting covers: ROAS, cost per booking, CTR, conversion rate, reach
- Channel-level reporting via GA4, Looker Studio, and platform native dashboards
- Reports structured for marketing leadership — clear, visual, conclusion-led

---

## Briefing Property Teams and Stakeholders
- Properties are required to supply: approved rates and availability, any property-specific photography or video, sign-off on offer details
- Asset requests should be sent early with clear specifications to allow adequate lead time
- Where possible, standardise asset requests so properties know exactly what is needed and in what format

---

## Reporting Format Preferences
- Clear and visual — Looker Studio dashboards preferred for ongoing reporting
- Executive summaries for leadership: lead with the result, then the context
- Campaign wrap reports: structured as brief recap, performance vs KPIs, key learnings, recommendations

---

## Templates and Frameworks
- Campaign brief template: detailed multi-section format (see structure above)
- Reporting template: Looker Studio dashboard per campaign/channel
- Asset request template: standardised list of required formats and specs for property teams

---

## Output Format Preferences
- Briefs: detailed multi-section documents
- Reports: visual dashboards (Looker Studio) plus written executive summary
- Creative: Adobe Creative Suite and Canva, sized per platform spec — Canva used for file sharing and collaboration
- Internal comms: Microsoft Teams and Outlook
