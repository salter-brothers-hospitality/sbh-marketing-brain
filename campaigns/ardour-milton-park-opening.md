# Campaign: Ardour Milton Park Bowral — Opening / Launch

## Objective
Drive awareness and bookings for the opening of Ardour Milton Park Bowral, the flagship Ardour Hotels & Estates property. Launched February 2026.

## Target Audience
- Cold audiences: luxury travellers within drive distance of Southern Highlands (Sydney, Canberra)
- Retargeting: website visitors, engaged social audiences, Worlds Apart platform visitors

## Key Message
An iconic Southern Highlands estate reimagined. Heritage restored, luxury renewed. The flagship of Ardour Hotels & Estates is now open.

## Offer Context
Opening offer: complimentary glass of Taittinger Champagne, $100 hotel credit, breakfast for two, 12pm late checkout. Valid for stays from 2 February 2026.

## Channels
- Meta (Ardour Milton Park property ad account)
- Quantcast (programmatic display)

## Creative Direction
- Ardour brand tone: confident, sensory, specific
- Em dashes permitted
- No platitudes, no "iconic" self-reference
- Lead with the estate's character and the opening moment
- Visual: heritage architecture, gardens, dining, interiors

## Timeline
- Launch: February 2026
- Status: Active

## Stakeholders
Digital Marketing Manager, Marketing Manager — Ardour & Independents, Talk Agency (regional Meta/Google), Group Director of Marketing Performance
