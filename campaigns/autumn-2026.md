# Campaign: Autumn 2026

## Objective
Drive bookings across Spicers Retreats properties during the Autumn shoulder season. Promote the Autumn extras package alongside Stay Longer & Save and Early Bird offers.

## Campaign Dates
- Campaign live: now — 30 April 2026
- Stay dates: 1 March 2026 — 31 May 2026

## Target Audience
- TOF (cold): brand-first awareness audiences
- Retargeting (warm): campaign-specific and offer-led messaging

## Key Message
TOF: Brand-first — Spicers Retreats in Autumn. Atmosphere, setting, season.
Retargeting: Autumn extras package. Stay Longer & Save. Early Bird.

## Offers in Play
- Autumn extras campaign package
- Stay Longer & Save
- Early Bird

## Channels

### Central (brand level)
- TikTok — TOF brand-first creative
- StackAdapt — programmatic, TOF and retargeting
- Teads — TOF brand-first

### Regional (property level, managed by Talk Agency)
- Meta — Autumn extras, Stay Longer & Save, Early Bird per property
- Google Ads — Autumn extras, Stay Longer & Save, Early Bird per property

## Creative Strategy
- TOF audiences: brand-first ads — Spicers Retreats in Autumn, seasonal atmosphere and setting. No offer messaging.
- Retargeting audiences: campaign-specific and offer-led — Autumn extras, Stay Longer & Save, Early Bird. Clear CTA.
- Spicers tone throughout: warm, grounded, specific. No hyperbole.
