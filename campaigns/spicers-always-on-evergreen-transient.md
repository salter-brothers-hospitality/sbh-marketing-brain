# Campaign: Spicers Retreats Always-On Evergreen Transient

## Objective
Drive transient room bookings for Spicers Retreats at a brand level. Top-of-funnel awareness and bottom-of-funnel conversion on an ongoing basis.

## Target Audience
Spicers Retreats brand audience — Julie and Sam personas. Cold and retargeting audiences.

## Channels
- Meta (Facebook + Instagram) — brand level
- TikTok — brand level

## Campaign Type
Always-on evergreen. Not tied to a seasonal campaign or specific offer. Brand-first creative and messaging.

## Creative Direction
- Elevated, evocative, factual — not vague
- No comparative or correlative structures
- Reels/video primary format
- Strong hooks, text on screen
- Audience-first content — emotional and experiential, not generic lifestyle

## Status
Always-on. Running continuously.

## Stakeholders
Digital Marketing Manager, Senior Content Marketing Manager, Content Marketing Manager
