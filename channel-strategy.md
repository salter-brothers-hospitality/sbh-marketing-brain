# Channel Strategy and Marketing Stack

## Channel Overview

---

### Paid Social

**Platforms:** Meta (Facebook + Instagram), TikTok, Pinterest

**Goal:** Drive direct bookings and top-of-funnel awareness across all SBH brands. Primary performance channel.

**Current status:** Active across multiple brands and properties. Meta is the primary paid social channel. TikTok and Pinterest are secondary.

**KPIs:** ROAS, cost per booking, CTR, CPM, reach, frequency, link clicks

**Tools:** Meta Ads Manager, TikTok Ads Manager, Pinterest Ads

**Notes:**
- Cold audiences: elevated, evocative, factual copy — not vague
- Retargeting audiences outperform cold audiences, particularly on Worlds Apart campaigns
- Meta ad structure: each property runs its own ad account. Campaigns are segmented by objective (e.g. conversions, awareness) or seasonal campaign. Ad sets break down audience targeting and ad content within each campaign.
- Pinterest and TikTok: advertising runs at brand level, not property level
- Headlines: straightforward, no comparative or correlative structures
- Descriptions: short, CTA-focused
- Avoid: "Whether X or Y", "From X to Y", "It's not X, it's Y" constructions
- Creative: Reels/video primary format for organic; static and video both used in paid
- Coolcations campaign is an active multi-property Meta campaign across Spicers properties

---

### Paid Search

**Platform:** Google Ads

**Goal:** Capture high-intent search traffic. Direct booking conversion.

**Current status:** Active.

**KPIs:** ROAS, conversion rate, cost per conversion, impression share, Quality Score

**Tools:** Google Ads, Google Tag Manager, GA4

---

### Programmatic Display

**Platforms:** StackAdapt, Quantcast, Teads

**Goal:** Awareness and retargeting across the open web. Supports paid social and search.

**Current status:** Active.

**KPIs:** CPM, CTR, view-through conversions, reach

**Tools:** StackAdapt, Quantcast, Teads

---

### Metasearch

**Platform:** Mirai

**Goal:** Capture travellers comparing rates across OTAs and direct booking channels. Drive direct bookings over OTA bookings.

**Current status:** Active.

**KPIs:** Direct booking share, cost per booking, impression share vs OTAs

**Tools:** Mirai

---

### Pay on Stay

**Platform:** Sojern

**Goal:** Performance-based channel — only pay when a booking converts to a stay. Lower risk acquisition.

**Current status:** Active.

**KPIs:** Bookings driven, cost per stay, ROI

**Tools:** Sojern

---

### Affiliate

**Platform:** Commission Factory

**Goal:** Partner-driven referral traffic and bookings through affiliate publishers.

**Current status:** Active.

**KPIs:** Referral bookings, commission cost, affiliate partner performance

**Tools:** Commission Factory

---

### Organic Social

**Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest (and others across portfolio)

**Goal:** Brand awareness, community building, top-of-funnel engagement. Supports paid performance through warm audience building.

**Current status:** Active across 57+ channels spanning hotel and restaurant properties.

**KPIs:** Reach, engagement rate, follower growth, Reels views, saves, profile visits

**Tools:** Adobe Creative Suite (content production), Meta Business Suite

**Notes:**
- Reels are the primary format
- Strong hooks (text on screen) essential
- Content batching workflow preferred
- Audience-first pillars — content should serve the audience, not just the brand
- Creative inspiration: Jacquemus-style originality and emotional impact over polished lifestyle imagery
- No generic openers, no directive copy starters, no "Nestled..."

---

### SEO

**Goal:** Organic search visibility for property and experience-led queries. Long-term direct booking channel.

**Current status:** Active and ongoing. Current focus includes Spicers Retreats landing pages (All-Inclusive, Blue Mountains, Peak Lodge, and others) and Spicers Balfour Hotel Meetings page refresh.

**KPIs:** Organic sessions, keyword rankings, page-level conversions, bounce rate, Core Web Vitals

**Tools:** Ahrefs, Visaible.ai, Surfer SEO, Google Tag Manager, GA4, BigQuery, Looker Studio, Sanity (CMS)

**Notes:**
- SEO copy written line by line with non-negotiables applied continuously
- Landing pages structured around specific search intent (e.g. all-inclusive retreats, Blue Mountains weekends, meetings and events)
- Australian spelling throughout
- No hyperbole, no pretentious language, no directive openers

---

### Email Marketing

**Goal:** Retention, re-engagement, and conversion across existing guest database. Package and offer promotion.

**Current status:** Active via Revinate. Migrating to Agilysys ADM.

**KPIs:** Open rate, click rate, conversion rate, revenue per email, unsubscribe rate

**Tools:** Revinate (current), Agilysys ADM (migration in progress)

---

### Analytics and Tag Management

**Goal:** Unified measurement across all digital channels. Attribution, reporting, and audience building.

**Tools:** GA4, BigQuery, Looker Studio, Google Tag Manager

**Notes:**
- GTM is essential for all tracking implementation
- BigQuery used for raw data storage and custom queries
- Looker Studio for reporting dashboards
- Standardised hotel naming conventions applied for data processing consistency

---

### Forms and Quizzes

**Goal:** Lead capture, campaign interactivity (e.g. Coolcations quiz), enquiry handling.

**Tools:** Jotform

**Notes:**
- Coolcations interactive quiz for Spicers Retreats was built in Gravity Forms
- Jotform is the current standard for new form builds going forward

---

### CMS

**Platform:** Sanity

**Goal:** Website content management across SBH properties.

**Tools:** Sanity

---

### PMS (Property Management Systems)

**Tools:** Siteminder, Mews, Agilysys (varies by property)

**Note:** PMS varies across the portfolio. Siteminder is the primary channel manager. Relevant for rate and availability data feeding into paid and metasearch channels.

---

## Growth Opportunities
- Retargeting audiences on Worlds Apart platform campaigns — consistently outperform cold audiences and warrant increased budget weighting
- SEO — ongoing opportunity to build organic landing page coverage across all properties and experience types
- Email — migration to Agilysys ADM and eventual move to Braze will unlock better segmentation and automation
- TikTok and Pinterest — currently secondary; opportunity to scale as creative production capacity grows

## Performance Challenges
- Cold audience paid social performance — broad targeting on Worlds Apart campaigns underperforms warm/retargeting audiences
- Cross-property attribution — with 57+ social channels and multiple booking paths, clean attribution is complex
- CRM maturity — current Revinate setup limits segmentation and personalisation; migration is a priority

## Measurement Framework
- Campaign level: ROAS, cost per booking, CTR, conversion rate
- Channel level: platform-specific KPIs (CPM, CPC, organic reach, keyword rankings, email open rate)
- Program level: total direct booking share, overall revenue contribution by channel, organic vs paid mix
