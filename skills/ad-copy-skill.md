# Skill: Ad Copy

## Purpose
Writing paid social and search ad copy across Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, and Google Ads for SBH brands.

---

## Output Format

### Meta Ads
- **Primary text:** 1–3 sentences. Lead with the hook. No filler.
- **Headline:** 4–7 words. Factual, specific, no comparative structures.
- **Description:** 1 sentence. CTA-focused.

### Google Ads
- **Headlines:** Up to 30 characters. 3 required, up to 15 available. Specific and keyword-relevant.
- **Descriptions:** Up to 90 characters. 2 required. Benefit-led, CTA at end.

### TikTok / Pinterest
- **Caption/copy:** Short, hook-first. Platform-native feel. Not repurposed Meta copy.

---

## Voice Rules by Brand

### Spicers Retreats
- Warm, grounded, specific
- Cold audiences: evocative and factual — no vague luxury language
- Retargeting: offer-led, subtle urgency, no desperation
- No directive openers (Discover, Experience, Celebrate)
- No comparative or correlative structures
- No em dashes
- Australian spelling

### Ardour Hotels & Estates
- Confident, sensory, permission-giving
- Specific details over generalisations (thread count, wine, birdsong)
- Em dashes permitted
- First person plural in general marketing (our, we, us)
- No platitudes, no "iconic" self-reference, no tired constructions
- Australian spelling

### Bannisters Hotels
- Coastal, relaxed, F&B-forward
- Mollymook: polished coastal — more refined
- Port Stephens: Barefoot Luxury — casual, sun-drenched, playful
- Rick Stein at Bannisters always referred to in full
- Shorter sentences, lighter touch
- Australian spelling

### Worlds Apart
- Editorial and aspirational
- Cross-brand without being generic
- Never promotional or discount-driven
- "A world of rewards that stand apart"
- Australian spelling

## Examples

### Good — Spicers Meta (cold, transient)
**Primary:** Twelve thousand acres of Queensland high country. No neighbours. No noise. Just the kind of stillness you forgot existed.
**Headline:** Spicers Hidden Vale — Book Direct
**Description:** Rooms available from $X. Book now.

### Bad — Spicers Meta (cold, transient)
**Primary:** Experience the ultimate luxury retreat getaway. Indulge in elevated dining and breathtaking natural beauty. Whether you're celebrating or simply escaping, Spicers has it all.
**Headline:** Luxury Awaits You
**Description:** Discover more here.

*Why it fails: Directive opener, hyperbole, comparative structure, overused words, vague.*

---

### Good — Ardour Meta (cold, opening)
**Primary:** An estate hidden in the Southern Highlands — restored, reopened, and ready. Ardour Milton Park Bowral is now open.
**Headline:** Ardour Milton Park Bowral
**Description:** Heritage restored. Book your stay.

### Bad — Ardour Meta (cold, opening)
**Primary:** Experience the iconic new Ardour Milton Park Bowral. Luxury reimagined in the heart of the Southern Highlands.
**Headline:** Discover Luxury Today
**Description:** Book now and indulge.

*Why it fails: Uses "iconic" and "reimagined" as platitudes, directive opener, vague.*

---

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Australian spelling throughout
- [ ] No em dashes (except Ardour)
- [ ] No directive openers
- [ ] No comparative or correlative structures
- [ ] No hyperbole or vague luxury language
- [ ] No overused words (scrumptious, delectable, indulge, elevated, culinary journey, beauty)
- [ ] Headline is factual and specific
- [ ] Primary text leads with a specific USP or setting detail
- [ ] Description has a clear CTA
- [ ] Copy matches audience temperature (cold = brand/evocative, retargeting = offer/CTA)
- [ ] Brand tone applied correctly (Spicers vs Ardour vs Bannisters vs Worlds Apart)
- [ ] No generic sign-offs or "here..." endings
