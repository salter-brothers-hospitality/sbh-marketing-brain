# Skill: Content Action Brief

## Purpose
Producing per-piece briefs for content published to the Worlds Apart journal at `/journal/`. The brief is the single source of truth for a writer before they start drafting — it captures audience, angle, outline, must-mention texture, and distribution intent in one place. Designed to be filled out in under 15 minutes per piece. Currently scoped to journal content; the template extends naturally to SEO destination guides and other editorial formats where needed.

---

## Output Format
Single-page document per piece. Tables where data is cleaner, prose where context is needed. No padding. If a field cannot be filled, the piece is not ready to brief.

## Quality Checklist (for the brief itself)
- [ ] Reader sentence is specific — describes a real person at a real stage, not a generic segment
- [ ] Thesis is one sentence and could not be confused with another piece's thesis
- [ ] "Why this angle, not the obvious one" is answered — not skipped
- [ ] Outline H2s are real section headers, not topic placeholders
- [ ] Must Mention list includes at least one specific name, dish, room, or detail
- [ ] Source material field points to actual files or systems, not "the brand guidelines"
- [ ] Internal links are named, not "relevant property pages"
- [ ] Primary CTA is decided before drafting begins
- [ ] Distribution intent covers at least three channels
- [ ] No field is left blank without explanation
