# Skill: Destination Guide

## Purpose
Writing and structuring SEO-driven destination guides for worldsapart.club at `/experiences/[region]`. These pages target informational keywords ("things to do [region]", "best restaurants [region]", etc.) and sit separately from accommodation and booking pages — no keyword cannibalisation risk. They serve as high-intent top-of-funnel entry points that connect regional discovery to SBH properties.

---

## Strategic Position

- **URL:** `/experiences/[region]` (e.g. `/experiences/hunter-valley`, `/experiences/blue-mountains`)
- **Primary keyword pattern:** `things to do [region]`
- **Secondary keywords:** `best restaurants [region]`, `[region] travel guide`, `weekend getaway [region]`, `what to do in [region]`
- **Intent:** Informational / inspirational — not transactional
- **Conversion path:** Regional discovery → SBH property interest → booking
- **Distinct from:** Accommodation pages (transactional), journal articles (editorial), campaign landing pages (offer-led)

---

## Page Structure

### 1. Hero
- Full-width landscape image — the region, not the hotel
- Region name as H1 (e.g. "Hunter Valley")
- One-sentence descriptor — factual, specific, grounded (e.g. "New South Wales' oldest wine region, two hours north of Sydney")
- Anchor navigation bar below hero linking to each section on the page

**Copy rules:**
- H1 is the region name only — do not add "Guide to" or "Things to Do in"
- Descriptor answers: what is this place, and how far is it from a major city
- No taglines, no directive openers

---

### 2. Region Snapshot (above the fold)
A compact, scannable block — not prose. Designed to answer "is this trip right for me?" in 10 seconds.

**Format: stat cards or a clean 2-column grid**

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Distance from Sydney | 2 hrs drive / 40 min flight |
| Distance from Melbourne | 8 hrs drive / 1 hr 20 min flight |
| Best time to visit | March–May (Autumn), September–November (Spring) |
| Known for | Wine, gourmet food, hot air ballooning, equestrian |
| Good for | Couples, groups, milestone celebrations |
| SBH properties here | Spicers Vineyards Estate |

**Copy rules:**
- No prose in this block — data only
- Travel times should reflect driving time from the nearest major capital city; include flying time if a regional airport is practical
- "Known for" should be factual activity categories, not brand language

---

### 3. Getting Here
Short prose section (3–4 sentences max) plus a scannable travel-options block.

**Prose:** One paragraph covering the most practical route. Mention road quality, scenic drive value if relevant, and key landmarks or routes en route.

**Travel options block (cards or simple table):**

| From | Drive | Fly | Train/Coach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sydney | 2 hrs via Pacific Motorway | 40 min to Cessnock Airport | 2.5 hrs coach |
| Melbourne | 8 hrs | 1 hr 20 min to Newcastle Airport | Not practical |
| Brisbane | 5 hrs | 1 hr 15 min to Newcastle Airport | Not practical |

**Copy rules:**
- Drive times should use standard traffic conditions — do not use Google Maps "fastest route" if it's not the obvious road
- Only include flight/rail if genuinely practical — don't pad
- No promotional language — this section is purely functional

---

### 4. Experiences by Category
The largest content section. Structured as tabbed or sectioned cards — guests can scan by interest type rather than reading everything.

**Categories (use only the ones that are genuinely present in the region):**
- Food & Wine
- Nature & Outdoors
- Arts & Culture
- Wellness
- Adventure
- Family

**Each category:** 4–6 experience cards

**Each card contains:**
- Image (landscape, 16:9)
- Experience name (H3)
- 2–3 sentence description — specific, factual, no hyperbole
- Optional: "Book at [SBH property]" CTA if the experience is directly accessible from or bookable through an SBH property
- Optional: External link to venue/operator (opens in new tab)

**Copy rules per card:**
- Name the experience or venue specifically — not "a local winery" but "Brokenwood Wines"
- Descriptions answer: what is it, what makes it worth going, and any practical note (booking required, seasonal, etc.)
- No directive openers on card descriptions
- Do not pad to 6 cards if only 3 genuinely belong in the category

---

### 5. Insider Picks
A curated short-list of 4–6 recommendations from an SBH insider perspective. These are specific, opinionated, and first-person adjacent in tone — closer to a concierge recommendation than a review.

**Format:** Numbered list or vertical card stack (not a grid — these deserve more space)

**Each pick contains:**
- Venue or experience name
- One sentence on what it is
- One sentence on why it's worth going — the specific hook
- Optional practical note (reservation required, seasonal hours, etc.)

**Example:**

> **Brokenwood Wines**
> One of the Hunter's most consistent producers, known for its Graveyard Vineyard Shiraz.
> Go for a cellar door tasting on a weekday when it's quieter — the team will walk you through the full lineup without rushing.
> Reservations recommended for groups of four or more.

**Copy rules:**
- Do not use "hidden gem", "must-visit", "world-class", or any superlative that can't be substantiated
- Each pick should have a distinct reason for being on the list — not five wineries with identical copy
- Tone is warm and specific — concierge, not brochure

---

### 6. Where to Stay
SBH properties in or near the region. This is the commercial section of the page — integrate it naturally, do not make it feel like an ad.

**Format:** Property cards (2–3 properties max per region in most cases)

**Each property card contains:**
- Property image
- Property name
- 2-sentence description — lead with the setting and what's distinct about the property
- Key amenities (3–4 bullet points — specific, not generic: "In-house cellar door and wine blending experience" not "great food and wine")
- "View property" and "Check availability" CTAs

**Copy rules:**
- Apply the relevant brand voice (Spicers, Ardour, Bannisters, Independents) per property
- Do not cross-promote brands that are not present in the region
- "Check availability" CTA should link to the booking engine for that property
- Do not use "nestled", "situated", or any generic location opener

---

### 7. Practical Guide
Compact reference section. Designed to be scannable — not read top to bottom.

**Sub-sections:**

**When to Go**
Seasonal breakdown in a simple table or three short paragraphs (one per recommended season). Cover: weather conditions, what's on, any seasonal events worth noting.

**What to Pack**
A short bullet list — 5–8 items relevant to the region and season. Not generic ("comfortable shoes") — specific where possible ("a warm layer for cellar door evenings in Autumn").

**Getting Around**
One short paragraph. Answer: do you need a car? Are there tour operators or shuttles? Is it walkable within the main town/village?

**Local Tips**
3–4 bullet points. Practical, specific, and genuinely useful — not "enjoy local produce" but "most cellar doors close by 5pm; book ahead for Sunday tastings during peak season."

---

### 8. FAQ
Targets long-tail keywords. 4–6 questions per guide.

**Question format:** Written as a guest would actually search — conversational, specific.

**Good examples:**
- "Is Hunter Valley worth visiting in winter?"
- "How many days do you need in the Hunter Valley?"
- "What is Hunter Valley known for besides wine?"
- "Is the Hunter Valley good for a girls' trip?"

**Answer format:** 2–4 sentences. Factual, direct, specific. No padding.

**Copy rules:**
- Do not write FAQs as "frequently asked" filler — each question must have genuine search intent behind it
- Answers should be complete enough to be useful without a click — Google will surface these in featured snippets
- Do not answer with a booking CTA — FAQ answers are informational

## SEO Requirements

- Title tag: `Things to Do in [Region] | Worlds Apart` — ≤60 chars
- Meta description: 1–2 sentences, informational, include primary keyword — ≤155 chars
- H1: Region name only (e.g. "Hunter Valley")
- H2s should map to section names and include secondary keywords naturally where they fit
- Internal links: link to SBH property pages, relevant journal articles, and where applicable to `/experiences/` pages for nearby regions
- Image alt text: descriptive and specific — not "beautiful landscape"
- Schema: see skills/schema-skill.md. Destination guides use CollectionPage + TouristDestination + ItemList + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList. LocalBusiness is deliberately excluded — it belongs on property pages, not the region guide
- Canonical: self-referencing

## Quality Checklist

**Content**
- [ ] Region Snapshot data is factually verified (drive/fly times, seasonality)
- [ ] Experiences are specific — named venues, not categories
- [ ] Insider Picks have a distinct "why" for each recommendation
- [ ] Property cards use brand-appropriate voice and are not generic
- [ ] FAQ questions reflect genuine search intent
- [ ] Practical Guide is scannable — no padding

**Copy**
- [ ] Australian spelling throughout
- [ ] No em dashes (except Ardour property descriptions)
- [ ] No directive openers (Discover, Experience, Explore)
- [ ] No generic openers (Nestled, Situated, Set amidst)
- [ ] No hyperbole or overused words (elevated, indulge, culinary journey, world-class)
- [ ] No comparative or correlative structures
- [ ] Factual USPs over vague emotional claims

**SEO**
- [ ] Title tag ≤60 chars and includes primary keyword
- [ ] Meta description ≤155 chars
- [ ] H1 is region name only
- [ ] H2s are section-mapped and include secondary keywords naturally
- [ ] Internal links to property pages included
- [ ] Image alt text descriptive and specific
- [ ] Word count within 1,200–1,800 target (excluding nav, cards, stat blocks)

**UX**
- [ ] Anchor navigation present below hero
- [ ] Experiences section uses tabs or sectioned layout — not a wall of text
- [ ] Region Snapshot is a stat block, not prose
- [ ] Property CTAs are visible and link correctly
- [ ] Mobile layout reviewed (stat cards, experience cards, FAQ accordion)
