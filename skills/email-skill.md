# Skill: Email Marketing

## Purpose
Writing email campaigns and offer communications across SBH brands, currently delivered via Revinate and migrating to Agilysys ADM.

---

## Output Format

### Subject Line
- Under 50 characters where possible
- Direct, curiosity-led, or offer-led
- No clickbait, no all-caps, no excessive punctuation
- One emoji permitted for Ardour subject lines (assists open rate)
- No emoji for Spicers or Bannisters unless brand guidelines evolve

### Preview Text
- 40–90 characters
- Complements the subject line — adds context, doesn't repeat it
- Treat as a second subject line

### Body
- Short opening — acknowledge the reason for the email in one sentence
- Lead with the offer or key message by the second sentence at the latest
- Prose paragraphs, not bullet lists (unless listing package inclusions)
- One primary CTA per email — clear, specific, and placed before the fold where possible
- Secondary CTA acceptable at the bottom
- Sign-off: warm but not over-familiar

---

## Voice Rules by Brand

### Spicers Retreats
- Warm, personal, grounded
- Offer-led without feeling like a sales pitch
- Subtle urgency — "available until X" not "don't miss out"
- Contractions encouraged
- No directive openers, no hyperbole, no overused words
- No em dashes
- Australian spelling

### Ardour Hotels & Estates
- Confident, sensory, personal
- First person to named guests where personalisation is available
- Em dashes permitted
- One emoji in subject line permitted
- No platitudes, no "iconic" self-reference
- Australian spelling

### Bannisters Hotels
- Relaxed and coastal
- F&B-forward where relevant — lead with dining when it's a dining offer
- Rick Stein at Bannisters always referred to in full
- Lighter and more casual than Spicers
- Australian spelling

### Worlds Apart
- Editorial and aspirational
- Cross-brand offers framed around the rewards program
- "A world of rewards that stand apart"
- Not promotional or discount-driven in tone

## Examples

### Good — Spicers offer email subject line
Autumn at Spicers. Rates from $X per night.

### Bad — Spicers offer email subject line
🍂 Don't Miss Our AMAZING Autumn Escape Deal!

*Why it fails: Emoji on Spicers, all-caps, exclamation mark, "amazing", "don't miss" urgency.*

---

### Good — Ardour opening paragraph
Milton Park is open. The Southern Highlands estate has been restored — thoughtfully, carefully, and with an eye for the details that make a stay worth remembering. We'd love to welcome you.

### Bad — Ardour opening paragraph
We are delighted to announce the grand opening of the iconic Ardour Milton Park Bowral. Experience elevated luxury in the heart of the Southern Highlands.

*Why it fails: Overly formal, "delighted to announce", "iconic", "elevated", "experience" as directive opener.*

---

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Subject line under 50 characters
- [ ] Preview text complements subject line, doesn't repeat it
- [ ] Key message or offer appears by the second sentence
- [ ] One primary CTA — clear and specific
- [ ] Australian spelling throughout
- [ ] No em dashes (except Ardour)
- [ ] No directive openers
- [ ] No hyperbole or overused words
- [ ] Urgency is subtle, not desperate
- [ ] Brand tone applied correctly
- [ ] Emoji rules observed per brand
- [ ] Sign-off is warm but not over-familiar
- [ ] Personalisation tokens checked if used (first name, property, past stay)
