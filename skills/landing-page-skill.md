# Skill: Landing Page Copy

## Purpose
Writing conversion-focused landing page copy for SBH packages, offers, and campaigns. Distinct from SEO landing pages — these pages are typically campaign-specific or offer-driven rather than keyword-optimised.

---

## Output Format

### Page Structure
1. **Hero headline** — specific, benefit-led, not a tagline. Answers "what is this?"
2. **Sub-headline or intro paragraph** — 1–2 sentences. Expands on the headline. Gives the guest a reason to keep reading.
3. **Offer / inclusions section** — what's in the package. Specific. Prose or a clean list depending on complexity.
4. **Experience section** — what the stay or experience actually feels like. Sensory and specific. This is where brand voice carries most weight.
5. **Property context** — brief grounding in the setting. Not a full property description — just enough to place the guest.
6. **CTA section** — clear, specific, one action. Booking dates, rate, and any conditions.
7. **FAQ (optional)** — for packages with complexity (all-inclusive, babymoon, C&E). Keep answers specific — no vague reassurances.

---

## Voice Rules
- Australian spelling throughout
- No em dashes (except Ardour)
- No directive openers (Discover, Experience, Explore)
- No generic openers (Nestled, Situated, Set amidst)
- No hyperbole
- No overused words (scrumptious, delectable, indulge, elevated, culinary journey, beauty)
- No comparative or correlative structures
- No prose ending with "Here..."
- Factual inclusions over vague promises
- Tone: warm, grounded, specific

---

## Page Types

### Package / Offer Landing Page
- Lead with the package name and what it includes
- Be specific about inclusions, stay dates, booking window, and value
- Include a CTA early — don't make the guest scroll to find out how to book
- Avoid making the page feel like a brochure — it should feel like a direct conversation

### Campaign Landing Page (e.g. Coolcations, Autumn)
- Lead with the campaign idea, not the brand
- Connect the seasonal or conceptual hook to a specific offer
- Multiple properties can appear on one page — give each a distinct identity, don't use the same copy across all
- CTA per property, not a single generic "book now"

### All-Inclusive / Retreat Package Page
- Inclusions are the conversion driver — be exhaustive and specific
- Guests booking all-inclusive want to know exactly what's covered
- FAQs should address the specific questions this audience has (arrival time, dietary requirements, what's not included)

---

## Examples

### Good — Hero headline (Spicers Sangoma, all-inclusive babymoon)
A private mountain retreat. Everything included. Just the two of you.

### Bad — Hero headline (Spicers Sangoma, all-inclusive babymoon)
Indulge in the Ultimate Babymoon Experience at Spicers Sangoma Retreat

*Why it fails: "Indulge", "ultimate", directive opener, hyperbole.*

---

### Good — Inclusions paragraph (Spicers Autumn package)
The Autumn Extras package includes accommodation, a three-course dinner for two, a bottle of wine on arrival, and a late checkout at noon — so you have no reason to rush.

### Bad — Inclusions paragraph (Spicers Autumn package)
Elevate your Autumn escape with our incredible Autumn Extras package. Featuring luxurious dining, indulgent extras, and so much more, this offer is the perfect way to celebrate the season.

*Why it fails: "Elevate", "incredible", "indulgent", "so much more" (vague), "perfect way to celebrate" (generic).*

---

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Hero headline is specific — answers "what is this?" without being a tagline
- [ ] Offer inclusions are complete and specific
- [ ] CTA appears early and is clear
- [ ] Australian spelling throughout
- [ ] No em dashes (except Ardour)
- [ ] No directive or generic openers
- [ ] No hyperbole or overused words
- [ ] No comparative or correlative structures
- [ ] No prose ending with "Here..."
- [ ] Experience section is sensory and specific — not brochure-ish
- [ ] FAQs (if included) are specific to the package, not generic reassurances
- [ ] Each property on multi-property pages has distinct copy
- [ ] Brand tone applied correctly per brand
