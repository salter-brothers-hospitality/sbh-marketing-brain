# Team and Key Contacts

## Organisational Structure Overview
SBH marketing operates across two layers: Central Marketing (brand company level) and Cluster/On-Property Marketing. Central marketing is responsible for group-wide brand, digital, and campaign activity. Cluster and on-property teams drive local hotel marketing and PR.

---

## Central Marketing Team

### Group Director of Marketing Performance
- Senior leadership. Primary approver for all campaigns and marketing activity.
- All central marketing roles report to this position.
- Key stakeholder for strategy, budget, and performance reporting.

---

### Digital Marketing Director
- Oversees digital marketing function including the Digital Marketing Manager role.
- Key collaborator on paid media strategy, performance, and channel planning.

### Digital Marketing Manager (Tynan's role)
- Responsible for digital marketing, brand strategy, and content operations across the SBH portfolio.
- Owns paid media, organic social, SEO, analytics, and creative production.
- Reports to Digital Marketing Director.

---

### Marketing Manager — Spicers
- Brand-level marketing manager for Spicers Retreats.
- Key collaborator on Spicers-specific campaigns, content, and brand activity.
- Reports to Group Director of Marketing Performance.

### Marketing Manager — Ardour & Independents
- Brand-level marketing manager for Ardour Hotels & Estates and Independent Retreat Hotels.
- Key collaborator on Ardour and Independents campaigns.
- Reports to Group Director of Marketing Performance.

---

### PR & Comms Manager
- Manages public relations and communications across the SBH portfolio.
- Key contact for press releases, media enquiries, influencer activity, and PR placements.
- Reports to Group Director of Marketing Performance.

---

### Senior Content Marketing Manager
- Oversees content strategy and creative content direction.
- Reports to Group Director of Marketing Performance.

### Content Marketing Manager
- Content production and execution.
- Reports to Senior Content Marketing Manager.

---

### Graphic Designer
- Visual design and asset production.
- Reports into the Spicers Marketing Manager structure.

---

## External Partners

### Talk Agency
- External agency managing Meta Ads and Google Ads for regional property teams.
- Key contact for paid media execution at the cluster/property level.

---

## Cluster and On-Property Marketing

### Regional General Manager
- Oversees cluster and on-property operations.
- Regional Marketing Managers report to this role with a dotted line to the Group Director of Marketing Performance.

### Regional Marketing Manager — QLD Cluster
- In-market marketing manager for Queensland properties.
- Reports to Regional General Manager, dotted line to Group Director of Marketing Performance.
- Supported by a Regional Marketing Coordinator.

### Regional Marketing Manager — HV Cluster
- In-market marketing manager for Hunter Valley properties.
- Reports to Regional General Manager, dotted line to Group Director of Marketing Performance.
- Supported by a Regional Marketing Coordinator.

### Regional Marketing Manager — BM's Cluster
- In-market marketing manager for Blue Mountains properties.
- Reports to Regional General Manager, dotted line to Group Director of Marketing Performance.
- Supported by a Regional Marketing Coordinator.
